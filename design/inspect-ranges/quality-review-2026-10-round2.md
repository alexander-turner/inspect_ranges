## The round's 25th finding: R2-25, hijack console corroboration was unattainable on default guests (found by the re-run, fixed)

The round-2-strengthened hijack battery row (achieved AND `corroborated is True`) failed deterministically across three runs, the last under a bounded 30 s re-score window. The evidence record showed the console conjunct absent while dmesg carried the disclosed address throughout: default guest console loglevel (kernel.printk 4, err and worse) keeps the kernel's INFO segfault line and WARN `/dev/kmsg` writes off the serial console, so the hijack type's `corroborated = console AND dmesg` was structurally unattainable on default-configured guests. The round-1 check asserted `achieved` only, so the round-1 README prose overclaimed and the gap predates this round; the strengthened check did its job by exposing it. Fix (user-decided shape): `HijackType.setup` raises the target's console loglevel (`dmesg -n 7`, root) before any plant state and refuses loudly if the raise fails, with CI pins for the ordering and the refusal. The re-run went 22/22 with the row corroborating on the first score, confirming the diagnosis (the earlier failures were purely the printk filter, not delivery lag).
