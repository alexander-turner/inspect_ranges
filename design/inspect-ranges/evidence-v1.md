# Evidence v1: T2 capture, the tamper-evident sink beside the eval log, and in-guest telemetry export, in six slices

*Phase plan for the evidence track, successor to [range-host-v1](range-host-v1.md). It builds the T2 observer layer that [challenges-scoring-integrity](challenges-scoring-integrity.md) trusts (per-segment pcap, continuous consoles, the plant record, verification results) and the T1 sink those observers are preserved at ("trusted while the instance is uncompromised; preserved at T1 as streamed"), plus in-guest telemetry export, making [host-provider](host-provider.md)'s "evidence is a declared output" (HP:104) and agent-containment backlog item 2 properties instead of plans. New code lives in `src/inspect_ranges/_evidence/` (files inside drop the underscore). The phase touches no daemon code and no wire schema: capture is host-side and telemetry export is poll-based through existing channel verbs, so the three codecs and their vectors are untouched by design. Lockstep rule throughout: implementation plus failure modes plus battery plus docs plus fresh-context review, or the slice is not done.*

## Scope

In, per the user's decisions (2026-10-14): capture in the range container, the sink abstraction with a local backend and an S3 backend, the hash-chained manifest format with an offline verifier, gap markers under backpressure, dual-clock stamping with offset records, collector heartbeats/watermarks for our own collectors, **in-guest telemetry export** (poll-based, through existing channel verbs; the user pulled this into scope rather than deferring it to the challenges feedback cycle), host-seam and provider wiring, doctor, docs. Out, recorded not implied: screenshots (no consumer); signed collector attestations (the named growth path that would upgrade T2 under instance compromise, scoring record line 89); a guest-initiated daemon streaming verb (growth path; polling is v1); the active prober and `corroborate()` (challenges-v1 consumes this sink; this phase produces it); Inspect sharding mid-run log sync (routed around: the sink is our own writer, so the recorded open dependency closes for evidence and remains open only for eval logs, which are Inspect's); retention and lifecycle policies (deployment guidance, not range semantics).

## Settled decisions

- **Full path in one phase** (user decision): the integrity claim ("the record received before a compromise cannot be rewritten") is only real once the off-instance leg runs; the S3 writer is small relative to the capture work. The local backend keeps dev and CI hermetic.
- **Evidence lives beside the eval log** (user decision): the per-sample location is `<evallogstem>.evidence/<sample_id>_<epoch>_<attempt>/`, derived from the eval log location, so durability inherits from where the logs go (local beside a local log in dev; the log's S3 bucket in prod, which Inspect supports natively) and anything that finds the log finds the evidence. Each attempt gets a fresh prefix and a fresh chain (write-once discipline across retries); the lease id is recorded in the seal so evidence, lease, and log cross-bind. Evidence is **not** stored in the Inspect sample store: it must stream off-instance as generated, independent of the scaffold's continued honesty, and pcap is GB-scale. The store carries the binding instead: evidence pointer, seal digest, and chain head, written at finalize, making log and evidence mutually verifying. In the separated topology the driver computes the location and hands the host a put-scoped destination at acquire. `INSPECT_RANGES_EVIDENCE_SINK` exists only as an override (different bucket or retention); the default derives from the log. Slice-1 verification item: confirm the active log stem is discoverable from provider context at `sample_init` (Inspect hooks/log API), with a log-dir-plus-run-id fallback documented if not.
- **Tamper evidence is a property of the format, not the store** (user decision): a per-sample evidence stream is a sequence of write-once chunks keyed `(sample, stream, seq)`; a manifest entry per chunk carries `{stream, seq, sha256, bytes, capture clock span, chain}` where `chain = sha256(prev_chain || entry bytes)`; a final **seal** record closes the chain with totals and the flush status (`complete` | `incomplete` | `reaped`). The manifest is itself written as sequence-numbered write-once parts (S3 has no append; re-putting a manifest object would be the rewrite we forbid). `inspect-ranges evidence verify <location>` replays the chain offline and reports the first break. **Scope of the format's own guarantee (chunk-1 verification finding, 2026-10-15):** the chain anchors everything at or below the seal (any in-chain rewrite, reorder, deletion, or hole is detectable from the location alone); the seal tier itself (its fields, its presence, the record's location, the writer set) is unanchored until someone holds the seal digest externally, which is exactly the sample-store binding written at finalize. `evidence verify --expect-seal <writer>=<digest>` supplies that binding and makes the full claim true for a binding-holder; without it, verify attests internal consistency only. Unsealed records are extendable as well as truncatable; the lifecycle makes unsealed-at-rest an anomaly (finalize and the reaper both seal). Store-side prevention (deny-overwrite bucket policy, Object Lock) is documented deployment guidance the claim does not depend on.
- **Write-only sink**: the writer keeps chain state in memory and never reads back, so the host's credential can be scoped to put-only. In the separated topology the host never needs read access to its own evidence.
- **Dual clocks with offset records** (user decision; closes the challenges doc's "clock basis" open question): capture-side records carry the range host's wall and monotonic clocks at capture; driver-side records (plants, submissions, latches) carry the driver's. Offsets are derived from paired records around events both sides observe (each `realize` stage report and each heartbeat: the host writes its emit time into the evidence stream, the driver writes its receive time into its own stream, bounding offset by one RTT) with no wire change and no daemon involvement; the co-resident topology degenerates to one clock. Corroboration tolerance math belongs to challenges-v1; this phase records the data it needs.
- **Overflow is never silent** (user decision): capture rotates and streams continuously; when the local buffer cap is hit, the oldest unstreamed chunk is dropped and an explicit **gap record** (stream, segment, time span, cause) joins the manifest in sequence. The completeness precondition sees gaps; only goals whose proof needed that window go indeterminate (per-goal, decided by scoring, not here). The forbidden behavior is a ring buffer losing data without a record.
- **In-guest telemetry export is poll-based through existing verbs** (user decision pulled it into scope; mechanism recorded here): a driver-side poller runs declared collector reads (`exec`/`read_file` against the collector guest, reads only, never carrying secret material guest-ward) on a cadence, streaming results to `telemetry/<collector>` with a per-poll watermark record (e.g. a journal cursor or log offset). Zero wire-schema churn and zero daemon rebuilds, matching the heartbeat-as-polled-verb precedent from range-host-v1; a guest-initiated streaming verb is the named growth path (the three-codec batch, if cadence ever proves insufficient). **The assurance claim, stated honestly: export upgrades the timeline, not the source.** Pre-compromise telemetry becomes immutable once streamed, and producer silencing shows as a watermark stall or poll gap rather than silent absence; the read remains T3 at origin, and the `detection` goal's completeness precondition is what consumes the watermarks. The challenges documents' "until telemetry export exists" wording is updated with this nuance at close-out.
- **What is captured**, stream by stream: `pcap/<segment>` (one capture per bridge, inside the range container's netns, rotated by size and time), `console/<guest>` (the existing `/scratch/console/<guest>.log` serial logs, tailed continuously instead of pulled on failure), `stages` (the realize stage stream with host emit times), `verify` (readiness/conformance results), `plants` (the oracle `PlantStore` record, driver-side, per [range-build](range-build.md)'s secrets-stay-driver-side rule), `session` (daemon session-id observations and `listener_restarts`, driver-side), `clock` (the offset pairs), `heartbeat` (capture liveness and watermarks for pcap and console capture, the same completeness machinery the `detection` goal demands of scenario collectors, applied to our own), `capture-stderr/<segment>` (the capture tool's own diagnostics, including the kernel-drop summaries tcpdump prints on SIGTERM; added in slice 2), `telemetry/<collector>` (the export poller, driver-side).
- **Who writes where**: the sink is a library with two backends behind one protocol; the capture side (range host: `LocalRangeHost` in-process, the applier in the separated topology) writes the capture streams; the driver writes the driver-side streams. Both address the same per-sample evidence location, different streams, independent chains per stream with the seal binding them. HP:104's "declared output" lands as a `HostCapabilities` field naming the sink the host writes to.
- **Lifecycle**: the evidence location opens at acquire (stage events are evidence from the first report), and `finalize evidence` — the sample state machine step HP:56 names — flushes, writes gap records for anything unflushed, seals, and writes the store binding, inside a budget so teardown is never hostage to a slow uplink; an unflushed remainder is recorded in the seal, never silently dropped. The reaper seals abandoned evidence (unsealed manifest plus reaped lease) with a `reaped` seal status so a dead driver still leaves a verifiable record.
- **Volume**: evidence volume joins the `WeightedAdmission` accounting (the provider-v1 design already names it); knobs `INSPECT_RANGES_EVIDENCE_BUFFER_MB` (local backpressure cap) and `INSPECT_RANGES_EVIDENCE_MAX_MB` (per-sample total, breach yields gap records plus a flagged seal).
- **Capability floor honesty**: packet capture inside the hardened range container likely needs `CAP_NET_RAW`, which is not in the measured eight-capability floor. The hardened-container discipline applies: add it only against an observed failure, record the justification beside the existing eight, and re-run the containment battery under the grown floor. If capture can run without it, prefer that; measured by the spike, not assumed. **Spike outcome (2026-10-15): the range container's floor stays at eight.** Capture runs in a `NET_RAW`-only sidecar sharing the range's netns; the observed refusal under exactly-eight is the justification for the sidecar's grant. The one pinned hypervisor-stack image gains `tcpdump` and `python3`; the binaries are inert in the range service itself, which holds no `NET_RAW` (the grant lives only on the sidecar), recorded here so the toolset addition to the escapee-landing container is a deliberate, justified choice and not drift.
- **Evidence never destabilizes the sample it observes**: evidence failure flags and gaps; it never throws into the sample lifecycle.
- **Battery CID band**: 30000 and up, documented beside the existing bands.
- No licensing commentary anywhere (standing policy).

## The slices

**Slice 1: sink, manifest format, verifier (pure CI).** `_evidence/sink.py` (the `EvidenceSink` protocol, `LocalDirSink`), `manifest.py` (chunk/entry/gap/seal models, the chain), `reader.py` (verified iteration — the verifier and the battery share it), the `inspect-ranges evidence verify` CLI, the log-stem derivation with the `sample_id_epoch_attempt` naming (and the Inspect-API verification item resolved here). Acceptance: round-trip of multi-stream writes through verify; the hostile battery mutates a stored record every way we claim to detect (chunk bytes, chunk order, a deleted chunk, a truncated tail, a re-put manifest part, a forged gap record out of sequence) and verify names the first break for each; the writer provably never issues a read (backend call log asserted); seal statuses round-trip; two attempts of one sample land in disjoint prefixes with independent chains. Failure modes: backend write errors mid-stream (buffered, retried, eventually a gap record, never an exception into the capture path); interleaved writers to one location stay per-stream isolated. Review weights: the chain actually covering entry metadata (a chain over chunk digests alone would let an attacker rewrite timestamps and spans); gap records being inside the chain, not beside it.

**Slice 2: capture in the range container (devbox, spike-informed).** Per-bridge pcap with rotation, console tailing, the stage/verify streams via the existing `on_stage` seam, capture heartbeats and watermarks, dual-clock stamping, the backpressure path. Acceptance: a booted two-segment range yields per-segment pcap in which a known cross-segment flow appears exactly on the segments it crossed (and not on others — per-segment capture is the point); console streams carry boot output continuously, not only on failure; forced backpressure (throttled sink) produces dropped-oldest chunks with in-chain gap records and an unbroken verify; capture heartbeats gap when the capture process is killed, and the kill is visible in the stream rather than inferred; the capability-floor outcome recorded either way. Failure modes: tcpdump dying mid-sample (heartbeat gap plus restart with a gap record, bounded restarts); a guest flooding a segment (rotation and caps hold, the range stays healthy — evidence pressure must never destabilize the sample it observes); clock step on the host (monotonic spans keep ordering sane; wall jumps recorded in the clock stream). Review weights: capture living strictly outside every VM (nothing guest-side, nothing on the agent's segment that wasn't already there); the one-process-per-bridge resource bill against the spike's density numbers.

**Slice 3: the S3 backend and the off-instance leg (devbox).** `_evidence/s3.py`: write-once object per chunk and per manifest part under the per-sample prefix, no multipart complexity until chunk sizes force it, retries with the house tenacity posture, put-only credential proven (the battery runs the writer under a deny-everything-but-PutObject policy). Acceptance: a live range's evidence lands off-instance as generated (watermark lag measured and recorded, not assumed "streaming"); verify runs against the S3 location from a different machine than the one that wrote it; the deny-overwrite deployment guidance written and tested against (a re-put attempt fails under the documented policy). Failure modes: uplink loss mid-sample (local buffering until the cap, then the slice-1 gap path; recovery resumes the sequence with no chain break); credential expiry mid-sample (same path, loudly logged); S3 ordering stated honestly (verify orders by key, never by list order).

**Slice 4: in-guest telemetry export (devbox).** `_evidence/telemetry.py`: the driver-side poller against a declared collector spec (poll command or paths, cadence, watermark extraction), existing channel verbs only, streaming to `telemetry/<collector>` with per-poll watermark records. Battery against a real Linux guest (journald or a file-based log on a target); Splunk/Windows collector integration deferred to the challenges work (recorded). Acceptance: events emitted on the guest appear in the stream with watermarks advancing; killing the in-guest producer shows as a watermark stall, not silent absence; poll failure (daemon down, simulated compromise) shows as poll-gap records; reads only — no secret material transits guest-ward, asserted the same way the oracle-residue checks assert it. Failure modes: watermark regression (guest tampering with the log — recorded as an anomaly record, never a crash); oversized poll output (byte caps, truncation recorded in the stream).

**Slice 5: seam wiring and the VM battery (devbox).** Provider: the evidence location keyed into acquire, the store binding (pointer, seal digest, chain head) written at finalize, `finalize evidence` in the sample state machine before destroy with the flush budget, admission accounting, reaper sealing. Host seam: `HostCapabilities` declares the sink; the applier owns capture-stream writing in the separated topology (driver streams stay driver-side), `LocalRangeHost` in-process. Battery (`design/spikes/evidence-v1/`, house conventions, CID band 30000+): the headline demonstrations — a live sample generating cross-segment traffic, `kill -9` of the driver mid-sample, the reaper reclaims, and the evidence written **before** the kill verifies intact off-instance with a `reaped` seal (the claim we made to the external reviewer, run for real); the same with a mid-sample simulated compromise window (everything streamed before the window verifies regardless of anything done on the instance after it); backpressure under a real boot-storm sample; the separated topology writing through the applier; wall-clock cost of capture on the 4-VM enforced-ready baseline recorded with the budget stated in advance (the ~50 s number must not grow materially). Review weights: no path where teardown waits unboundedly on flush; no path where evidence failure fails a healthy sample; battery non-vacuity (wall-time rule; a gather-window overlap check proves nothing).

**Slice 6: docs, doctor, record close.** Docs: an Evidence page stating the trust story honestly (T2 trusted while the instance is uncompromised, preserved at T1 as streamed; what tamper-evidence does and does not claim; the telemetry-export claim; the gap and seal semantics; the clock records; the knob table; the deployment guidance for store-side prevention). Doctor: a sink group (configured-sink reachability and writability probe, local buffer dir health, a loud warning when evidence resolves to instance-local storage under an untrusted-model isolation claim). CHANGELOG; this record's ledger and non-goals closed against reality; [challenges-scoring-integrity](challenges-scoring-integrity.md) and the challenges overview updated where this phase answers their open items (clock basis; the T1 sink exists; the `detection` assurance wording).

## Conformance trajectory

Slice 1: the hostile mutation battery over `LocalDirSink`. Slice 3: the same battery over S3 plus the cross-machine verify. Slice 5: the kill -9 and compromise-window demonstrations on live ranges, sealed and verified off-instance.

## Logging and debuggability

Every record carries lease id and sample id; chunk writes log at debug (stream, seq, bytes, lag), gap records at WARNING with cause, the seal as one summary line (streams, chunks, bytes, gaps, status, worst watermark lag). `evidence verify` output is designed for incident use: first chain break named with its neighborhood, per-stream coverage spans, gap inventory. The capture processes log their own restarts; the telemetry poller logs per-poll outcomes at debug and anomalies (watermark regression, truncation) at WARNING. A sample's evidence trouble is diagnosable from the manifest alone, without host access — that is the point of the format.

## Review discipline

Every slice closes with a fresh-context review scoped to the slice diff, findings fixed in their own commits; an integrator-spawned fresh-context verification runs per chunk before any merge is proposed, with blocking findings demonstrated, not asserted. The slice-1 review reads the chain construction line by line against the mutation battery's coverage; the slice-5 review hunts for any path where evidence failure can alter sample outcome or teardown. The daemons, codecs, and wire vectors are untouched by design; every review verifies that.

## Non-goals and recorded follow-ups

Screenshots; signed collector attestations (growth path for T2-under-compromise); a guest-initiated telemetry streaming verb (growth path if polling cadence proves insufficient); Splunk/Windows collector integration (challenges work); the active prober and `corroborate()` (challenges-v1, consuming this sink); Inspect sharding log sync (eval logs only, external); retention policies; evidence-driven live dashboards.

Closed against reality (2026-10-15): every non-goal above stayed out. The phase touched no daemon, codec, or wire-vector path, verified independently in both chunk verifications. The clock-basis open question from the challenges work is answered by this phase: evidence records carry both clocks, and paired records around stage reports and heartbeats bound the offset, so corroboration comparisons carry a tolerance instead of a hidden sync assumption (the overview's open-questions list should be updated by its author). Residuals accumulated during the phase, each deliberate and documented at its point of use:

- `telemetry-meta/<collector>` joined the stream list (additive, slice 4, the `capture-stderr` precedent).
- The `plants` stream stays unwired until the challenges-v1 oracle exists; the `session` stream is wired (boot pins and ops re-pins), but `listener_restarts` is not, because no provider-path reply carries it today.
- Gap-cause phrasing widened from "data existed" to observation holes (`dropped=0` means unknown), consistent across capture and telemetry.
- The S3 put-only proof is client-layer scoped (IAM on the devbox denies minting a genuinely put-only credential) and the deny-overwrite bucket policy ships as written-but-site-applied guidance; the conditional-put refusal it enforces is store-proven (a real 412).
- The chunk-2 cross-machine verify was substituted with a scrubbed-environment subprocess (recorded in the battery README); slice 5 then verified real S3 records from the eval-log binding, and a true second machine remains the fleet-work follow-up.
- Store-binding writes outside the sample context (deferred teardown, `cleanup=False`) degrade to the INFO log mirror carrying the same values.
- The writer gained `checkpoint()` (review-forced: batching must never leave gap records uncommitted on a no-seal shutdown) and `TelemetryPoller.stop()` is safe under external task cancellation (chunk-2 verification finding, fixed in slice 5).
- Capture tooling (tcpdump, python3) lives in the one pinned hypervisor-stack image, inert in the range service (no `NET_RAW`; the grant is sidecar-only), recorded here and in the build/images requirements inventory.
