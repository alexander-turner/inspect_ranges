# Range host v1: the HostProvider seam and the one-host separated topology, in five slices

*Phase plan for the range-host track, successor to [provider-v1](provider-v1.md). It implements the `RangeHost`/`HostProvider` seam that [host-provider](host-provider.md) sketches (HP) and the host-plane semantics [range-channel](range-channel.md) specifies (RC): a lease store with identity and expiry, the independent reaper, a `LocalRangeHost` wrapping today's co-resident path, and the separated topology on one host via an applier endpoint speaking the already-implemented host-plane messages over a unix-domain-socket transport, consumed by a `RemoteRangeHost` and proven across two OS processes. New code lives in `src/inspect_ranges/_host/` (files inside drop the underscore) plus `_channel/uds.py`. The provider's injectable seams (`ProviderRuntime.up_fn`/`down_fn`/`render_fn`/`channel_factory`) are the wedge, so `sample_init`'s orchestration is touched minimally. Lockstep rule throughout: implementation plus failure modes plus battery plus docs plus fresh-context review, or the slice is not done.*

## Scope

In: the full one-host topology per the user's decision. Out, recorded not implied: TCP/mTLS transports, multi-host dispatch and applier-side CID allocation, granted-URL/registry image delivery (pre-seeded only, so nothing here prejudges the build/images architecture under review), evidence streaming, the host-side containment battery behind the verify gate, `isolation="instance"` (needs a real fleet provider per HP:26).

## Settled decisions

- **Bundle wire path**: `realize` scopes to "the digest names a bundle the applier can already reach" (one host, shared filesystem). The **bundle digest is sha256 of `manifest.json` bytes**, which render emits deterministically; no canonical definition existed before this record. The applier verifies the requested digest against the resolved bundle's manifest bytes before `verify_bundle` (HP:48). A bulk bundle-transfer verb is image-distribution-arc territory, deferred.
- **Heartbeat is a driver-initiated polled verb**: `MessageChannel.heartbeat()` sends the existing `PingRequest` to `@host` and expects the existing `Heartbeat` reply with the matching id. Zero wire-schema churn, zero daemon rebuilds (daemons never see `@host` traffic). HP:56's "periodic" is interpreted as driver-polled: a pull verb fits per-operation-connect and connectionless transports where an unsolicited push does not. A dedicated `HeartbeatRequest` kind is the recorded multi-host growth path (the three-codec batch pattern).
- **Realize gains a typed refusal**: an `ErrorReply` carrying the request's id before any stage report surfaces as `HostRefused(errno)`; wrong-id or post-stage error replies stay tamper. Applier admission refuses rather than queues: `EAGAIN` when full now (transient; a new `admission` stage joins `TRANSIENT_UP_STAGES` so layer-3 respins classify it), `ENOSPC` when the range alone exceeds host capacity (permanent). Queuing with progress-stall reports is the growth path; queued silence would blow the realize per-report budget.
- **Stage mapping** (the applier translates; `up.py` stage names stay): `verify-images` to `fetch`; `prepare`/`range-image`/`range-container` coalesce into one `construct` report (the strictly-increasing stage order forbids repeats; detail carries the underlying up stage); `guest-boot` to `boot`; `readiness` to `verify` then `ready`; `failed` detail begins with the `UpError` stage name, making the reverse mapping lossless for respin classification. `up()` gains an injectable `on_stage` callback threaded through its stage-log call sites with the JSONL byte-identical; the translation is a pure, CI-testable function.
- **Seam types, first definitions anywhere, deliberately minimal**: `SampleSpec{sample_id, task_name, spec_sha256, bundle_digest, totals}`, `HostCapabilities{image_delivery: "pre-seeded", forward_upstream: False, egress_grant: False}`, `HostFacts{arch, hostname, host_class}`. `RangeHost`/`HostProvider` are Protocols matching HP:83-91.
- **Lease store**: `leases.json` beside `cids.json` under the shared state dir, via a flock/atomic-write/extras-preserve helper extracted from `CidAllocator` (allocator behavior pinned byte-compatible by its existing tests). Record: opaque `lease_id` (uuid4 hex), project, task/sample ids, isolation claim, origin (`local` or `uds:<socket>`), bundle path and digest, staging, created, absolute expiry, `state` (`active` or `reaping`), host facts. Renewal is a CAS under the flock at TTL/3 (`INSPECT_RANGES_LEASE_TTL_S`, default 600); a lost CAS raises `LeaseLostError` and the sample fails loudly as lease loss. Renewal is an on-disk write in both backends: zero hot-path wire chatter (HP:108-110).
- **Expiry-mid-sample policy**: the reaper reclaims on expiry alone and never probes liveness. HP:60 makes the lease the contract and HP:61 forbids liveness-guessing; any looks-live grace reintroduces exactly the finally-dependence the reaper exists to remove. A healthy sample never expires because renewal is independent of sample activity; the only way to expire mid-sample is a dead or wedged driver, which is the reaper's job.
- **Reaper**: `inspect-ranges reaper` (one-shot sweep plus `--interval`), fresh-process, on-disk only. Sweep: under the flock, select expired or `reaping` leases, re-check expiry (a renewal may have landed), mark `reaping`, unlock; teardown outside the lock; then remove the lease and release the project's CID lease. Crash-resume via the `reaping` state. The destroy-before-release invariant from provider-v1 extends: nothing is freed before a successful teardown. For `uds:` leases the reaper prefers teardown through the applier socket (releasing its admission charge and relay map); on connect failure it falls back to direct `down` with a logged warning that a live applier's charge leaks until restart (recorded v1 risk).
- **Acquire ordering**: admission, CID lease, render (needs `cid_base`), bundle digest, `host_provider.acquire(SampleSpec)` (writes the lease, runs the doctor-readiness gate per HP:94, logs the isolation claim per HP:27, starts renewal), realize via the host's channel, session pins. The crash window before the host lease exists is covered by today's CID-lease-plus-registry findability. Destroy: teardown, CID release, host lease release, then staging/registry/admission.
- **UDS transport and preamble**: one listener serves both planes. `UdsTransport(socket, lease_id).connect(endpoint)` sends magic `IRH1`, the lease id, and the endpoint name, then waits for a one-byte routed-ack before returning the stream. Every routing failure (unknown lease, expired/reaping lease, unknown guest, guest dial failure) happens before any request bytes exist and surfaces as `ConnectionError` at connect, preserving the load-bearing "provably never left" semantics; after the ack, loss is recovered by same-id resend plus daemon dedupe exactly as today. Guest endpoints relay as pure byte pumps (never parsed) via `VsockTransport` dial, inheriting its pacing and backoff; guest names are lease-scoped so concurrent samples with identical names cannot cross. Socket mode 0600 in a 0700 dir, same-uid trust; TCP/mTLS deferred.
- **The applier endpoint** (`inspect-ranges applier --socket <path> --state-dir <dir>`): realize resolves the lease's bundle path, verifies the digest (refuses `EINVAL` on mismatch), enforces the grants convention (empty list means pre-seeded; non-empty refuses `ENOTSUP`), charges its own `WeightedAdmission` from the bundle's plan totals, runs `up` in a worker thread with the stage translation, and keeps an id-keyed replay log honoring the identical-replay contract for resumed streams. Teardown drives `down` and releases the charge and relay map. The applier runs the doctor-readiness subset at startup and refuses to serve unhealthy. Applier restart loses the replay log, relay map, and admission charges: a recorded v1 limitation, loudly logged, with disk-driven teardown (reaper, `cli_cleanup`) as the recovery.
- **Backends behind config**: `INSPECT_RANGES_HOST` (default `local`) selects `LocalRangeHost`, which wraps today's exact path adding only the lease, renewal, the cached-per-task doctor-subset gate, and the isolation log line, and is otherwise behavior-identical (proven, not asserted). `uds:<path>` selects `RemoteRangeHost`, which derives the provider seams: `up_fn` drives `channel.realize` and folds the stage stream back into `UpResult` or `UpError` (stage from the failed detail), `down_fn` is `channel.teardown`, `channel_factory` builds on `UdsTransport`. Both backends honestly claim `isolation="shared"`.
- **CIDs stay driver-leased** from the shared state dir in v1 (CIDs are baked into rendered bundles and render is driver-side); applier-side CID negotiation is a multi-host deferral.
- **Battery CID band**: 20000 and up, documented beside the existing bands.
- No licensing commentary anywhere (standing policy).

## The slices

**Slice 1: host-plane channel completion and the seam types (pure CI).** `MessageChannel.heartbeat()`; `HostRefused`; the `bundle_digest()` helper and `verify_bundle(expected_digest=)`; the `up()` `on_stage` callback; the pure stage-translation table; `_host/host.py` with the five seam types. Mocks: `FakeApplier` answers heartbeat and gains a refusal knob; hostile one-liners `heartbeat-wrong-kind`, `refusal-wrong-id`, `error-after-stage`. Conformance: a host-plane heartbeat test (RTT budget 1) and the overridable `realize_digest()` hook (the current host-plane tests hardcode a fake digest). Acceptance: heartbeat green on loopback and the latency mock within budget; refusals typed with the errno and every hostile variant tamper with the trace tail; `on_stage` ordering equals the JSONL for success and every stage-named failure; the translation table is table-driven over all up stages and statuses including coalescing and ready-is-never-first. Failure modes: a hostile applier answering heartbeat with a guest-reply kind; a refusal after a stage report (tamper: refusals are only legal as the first reply); an `on_stage` callback that raises (caught and logged, never altering `up`'s outcome). Review weights: the refusal change against the hardened realize loop (no tamper bypass; refusals never memoized as streams); the up refactor leaving the stage log byte-identical; heartbeat-over-ping recorded honestly with the successor named.

Acceptance: two real OS processes contending on `leases.json` mint disjoint leases and never corrupt extras; the renewal-versus-reap race pinned both ways (sweep re-check spares a just-renewed lease; a reaped lease refuses renewal with `LeaseLostError`); reaper ordering (down, CID release, lease removal) with a failed down leaving everything findable, and crash-resume proven by a two-invocation test; the default-local provider lifecycle behavior-identical to today plus one lease and one isolation log line per sample; every `sample_init` early exit releases the lease; `cleanup=False` leaves the lease with the reaper named in the recovery note. Review weights: any path freeing the lease before a successful down; the allocator extraction leaving `cids.json` byte-compatible; renewal-task cancellation on every sample exit path.

**Slice 3: UDS transport, applier endpoint, RemoteRangeHost (CI, two OS processes).** `_channel/uds.py`; `_host/applier.py` (unix server, preamble read under budget, the `@host` handler, the guest byte-pump relay, injectable `up_fn`/`down_fn`/guest-connector seams); the `applier` CLI; `_host/remote.py`. Failure modes: preamble garbage/overlength/timeout (close and log, never crash the server); half-close propagation both directions; pump cancellation leaking no fds; applier restart mid-sample (stated limitation; teardown from disk still works); digest mismatch refusing before any docker action; expired or reaping leases refused at the preamble. Review weights, the heaviest of the phase: the never-left property across the ack handshake (any path where `ConnectionError` can follow delivered bytes reintroduces double-runs; endpoint-side byte-count assertions required); reverse stage-mapping fidelity (every `UpError` stage round-trips through the failed detail); replay-log memory bounds; the relay never parsing guest frames.

## Lifecycle matrix (slice 4; CI shadows in slices 2 and 3)

`kill -9` of the driver mid-sample leaves the range up and the lease unrenewed; a fresh-shell reaper reclaims after expiry through the live applier with zero residue and the admission capacity returning. Forced expiry mid-sample reclaims a running sample and the driver fails loudly with `LeaseLostError` (the deliberate policy). Two concurrent samples through one applier hold distinct leases with non-vacuous boot overlap, identical guest names reaching their own guests, disjoint CID blocks, and independent teardowns. A failing down under `RemoteRangeHost` leaves lease, CID lease, and registry findable with nothing freed until a later pass completes. Heartbeat reads pending/booting during realize, ready after, and connect-refusal after teardown. The reaper-versus-renewal race holds both ways against the real store. An applier restart between samples serves the new sample cleanly while the old sample's teardown recovers from disk.

## Conformance trajectory

Slice 1: heartbeat and refusal over loopback and the latency mock within exchange budgets. Slice 3: the full portable suite with the host plane plus self_check through `RemoteRangeHost`, two processes, CI. Slice 4: both over the live applier on a booted range, self_check 44/44 at the empty pin.

## Logging and debuggability

Lease ids join the request-id and project correlation story: acquire, renew-failure, expiry, and reap each log one structured line carrying lease id and project. The applier logs per-connection routing decisions (lease, endpoint, verdict) and per-realize stage translations; heartbeat answers are never logged at INFO. The reaper's sweep summary is one line per lease with the outcome.

## Review discipline

Every slice closes with a fresh-context review scoped to the slice diff, findings fixed in their own commits. The slice-3 review reads the never-left analysis line by line. The wire vectors and codec files are untouched by design; a reviewer should verify that.

## Non-goals and recorded follow-ups

TCP/mTLS; multi-host dispatch and applier-side CID allocation; granted-URL and registry image delivery; evidence streaming; the host-side containment battery behind verify; a dedicated acquire wire verb and `HeartbeatRequest` kind (the three-codec batch, when multi-host arrives); applier replay-log durability across restarts; applier-side admission queuing; `isolation="instance"`.
