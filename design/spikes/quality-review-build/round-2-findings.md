## Non-blocking (fixed unless noted)

- F3-2 + F2-2 (found independently, both probed): `_retract_unproven` bypassed the per-range manifest lock, so a concurrent render's stamp could resurrect the retracted manifest from its read-modify-write window. Fixed: retraction moved into `manifest.retract_manifest`, a locked load-check-unlink that also matches the manifest's IDENTITY (`built_at` seen at resolve time), which closes F3-4's sibling case too (a concurrent build's fresh green manifest is not "prior" and is never deleted).
- F3-3 + F4-3: residual R1-B class: a BaseException escaping `up` (no disposal guarantee) destroyed staging under a likely-surviving range, and the bx phase rmtree'd its bundle even when the bx teardown failed. Fixed: non-UpError exits from `up` keep staging; the bx bundle survives a failed bx teardown so the retry hint makes sense; tests.
- F3-4: the same-spec concurrent-build refusal has a window the width of the behavioral phase (the bx project carries a different compose label). The sibling-manifest deletion half is closed by the identity-matched retraction; the window itself (interleaved stage logs, racing captures that fail closed at the gate) is RECORDED as a known limitation in the orchestrate docstring rather than locked, and a build-scope lock question is left with the parked concurrency notes.
- F3-5: `network-settle` and `uplink` failures landed no stage-fail record. Fixed: the BuildError arm logs before disposing; the uplink call site logs and re-raises.
- F4-1 (probed): the round-1 dpkg Status filter dropped held-but-installed packages (`hold ok installed`, the canonical pin for vulnerable-by-design versions), wrongly refusing good ranges. Fixed: match the status word; test row.
- F4-2 (probed): `version_matches` refused a verbatim-copied `+really` declaration against itself. Fixed: both sides bind to the post-`+really` version; test row.
- F4-4: `_retract_unproven` is not reached on BaseException (Ctrl-C mid-re-proof keeps the prior manifest). DECLINED by adjudication: an aborted proof yields no adverse information, the state equals never-having-started, and `checks_sha256` keeps the fast path invalidated; recorded here.
- F4-5: the round-1 uplink recheck could raise raw TimeoutExpired. Fixed: the recheck is guarded; the create failure stays the primary diagnosis.
- F4-6: `guest_build_key`'s `range_name=""` default on an exported API would silently reopen the collision class. Fixed: required keyword; tests updated to the production shape.
- F4-7: `validate_vars` double-reported a dirty-named required input. Fixed.
- F1-F3: `build --help` and the orchestrate module docstring still described the slice-2 subset ("verification and the manifest arrive with later slices"). Fixed to the six-phase reality.
- F1-F4 (probed): unreadable `challenges.yaml`/checks files crashed raw out of `checks_digest`. Fixed: wrapped into the `behavioral` stage refusal.
- F1-F5: `BuildOptions.settle_probe`/`settle_timeout_s` had no CLI flags, so fleets whose resolver answers only internal names could not repoint or disable the hardcoded `archive.ubuntu.com` gate. Fixed: `--settle-probe` (with `off`) and `--settle-timeout`.
- F1-F6: a kept range holds host-global vsock CIDs and a rebuild of an EDITED spec collides with no cause named. Fixed at the cheap end: the kept-range CLI message and building.qmd now connect `--keep` to `--cid-base`; a cid-conflict cause detector in guest-boot is left as a note.
- F2-3: `up --from-spec` (and the provider) dropped the refusal's remedy hint. Fixed for upcmd (hints render with the message); the provider's IssueError surface into inspect-ai is unchanged (its message names the condition; enriching `IssueError.__str__` globally was out of scope).
- F2-4: the provider-side gate (slice 6's headline) had zero unit coverage. Fixed: `resolve_config` refusal, swap, backings, and batch-cache tests.
- F4's doctor-assert nit (the near-tautological nameserver status check) stands as commented; the F2 declines (batch-cache span, realized-generation GC, stamp last-render-wins, orphan sidecar asymmetry, triple hashing) and F3 declines (gate-pass window, Ctrl-C-no-retraction, capture-overwrite fail-closed, bx efficiency) are recorded in the reviewers' reports and were not promoted.
