## Findings, ranked

### F1 (HIGH; class d, pin does not pin; probe-proven). The applier synthetic-failed-report pin is retired by fixture infidelity

- Test: `tests/test_host_applier.py:511-528` (`test_up_error_feeds_the_synthetic_failed_report`). Fixture: `tests/applier_fixture.py:154-161` (`fake_up`'s `fail-at-stage` injection).
- Production seam: `src/inspect_ranges/_host/applier.py:604-620` (the `except UpError` synthetic `(error.stage, "fail")` feed, plus the `except Exception` applier-internal arm), contract at `src/inspect_ranges/_host/stages.py:5`.
- Divergence: in real `up()`, `verify_bundle` raises at `src/inspect_ranges/_runtime/up.py:408` BEFORE the stage log (and its on_stage tee) is constructed at `up.py:448`, so a verify-bundle failure never reaches `on_stage`; the applier's synthetic feed is the only path to the driver-visible `failed` report. `fake_up` instead calls `on_stage(stage, "fail", ...)` and THEN raises `UpError`. The `StageTranslator` latches `_done` on the first fail (`stages.py:61-69`), so when the applier's synthetic arm runs, `translate` returns `None` and nothing is emitted: the pin passes entirely through the on_stage path, and the arm it is named for is behaviorally dead in every CI run (coverage shows the lines green). `fail-at-stage` is the only suite-wide path that raises `UpError` through the applier, so the arm is unexercised everywhere.
- What it could hide: if the synthetic feed regressed, a real pre-stage-log failure (unverifiable bundle, early prepare refusal) yields a realize stream ending with ZERO reports; the channel treats early end as loss and resends the same id until exhaustion, so the driver sees a slow transport-exhaustion error instead of the typed `failed` report, and the stage-to-UpError reverse mapping the provider's respin classification depends on is gone.
- Probe (sub-reviewer, mutation): `Applier._execute` replaced at runtime with a verbatim copy whose except arms set `failed = True` without feeding the translator or emitting; the pinning test still PASSED under mutation (baseline also passes). I independently confirmed the mechanism statically (ordering at up.py:408 vs 448; translator latch at stages.py:61-69; fixture feed at applier_fixture.py:157-161).
- Fix shape for triage (not applied): make `fake_up`'s pre-stage-log failure stages (`verify-bundle`) raise WITHOUT feeding `on_stage`, mirroring the real flow; keep fed-then-raised only for stages that genuinely reach the log.
